3rd position with Team Khatte Rasgulle

Polygenesis: VoltEdge, Delhi Technological University

IPI-DTU, 2 November 2026. Team of six.

The case
Design a battery-swapping network for electric two-wheelers in India that can carry a lifetime battery warranty and still make money.
Our answer
A separate battery bank owns the packs, riders buy a “virtual battery” with an 80% health floor, and the network starts in Bengaluru in 2027. Each ₹52 lakh hub pays back in about five years.
Our part
The whole deck, as a team. We presented it to the judges.
